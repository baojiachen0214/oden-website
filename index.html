TEXT, FILES AND FORMULAS — CONNECTED

Build the map.
Keep the evidence inside.

A PDF page, a research note, a formula: give each one a place in your map. Read the source where you use it, instead of separating the idea from its evidence.

01 / RESOURCE NODES

Put the source inside the node.

Drag in images, PDFs or Markdown as resource nodes, or attach a file above a text topic. Turn PDF pages and scroll enlarged Markdown cards on the canvas. Link originals or embed resources in your document.

02 / TWO WAYS TO EDIT

One document. Canvas or outline.

Edit the same topics and hierarchy in either view. Draft with rich text and formulas in the outline, then return to the canvas. Your map and writing stay in one document.

↗

A mind map can be a node.

Drop a supported mind map into your canvas as a preview card. See its structure in context, use Quick Look for detail, or open the original file in its app.

ƒ

Write formulas. Keep them editable.

TeX source stays with the topic. Use inline and display math, aligned derivations, matrices and cases, then return to the source to revise it.

◐

Style the line, too.

Choose connection shapes, widths, dashes and pressure curves. Set the whole map’s layout or give a branch its own structure, with automatic and free placement options.

FROM RESEARCH TO ACTION

More of your workflow, in one map.

01

Calendar & Reminders, connected

Turn a branch into tasks and track completion from its parent. Link leaf tasks to Reminders or schedule topics in Calendar; supported dates and changes sync both ways. Batch controls handle several topics together.

02

Present straight from the map

Present the whole map or one branch in a native window. Reveal the hierarchy step by step, keep speaker notes nearby, and open a selected resource in Quick Look.

03

Bring your existing maps

Import XMind, MindNode Classic, FreeMind, MindManager, OPML and more. Transfer supported topics, notes and resources; proprietary layouts and styles vary by format.

04

Send from Finder and other apps

Use Finder Quick Actions, macOS Services or the Share menu to send text, images and files to an open map. Finder thumbnails and Quick Look make saved maps easier to find. System extensions need to be enabled.

05

Group, connect and focus

Connect topics beyond the hierarchy, add summaries and background frames, group children by label, or fold and search branches to focus on one part of the map.

06

Make the controls yours

Customize shortcuts for canvas editing, writing, formulas and presenting. Work with a trackpad or mouse, and switch English or Chinese interfaces with native offline help.
