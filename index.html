01 / BEYOND WORDS

A place for your ideas.
And everything behind them.想法有了位置。
资料，也有了上下文。

Stop separating what you think from what you found. Build connections between words, documents, media, and whole maps. Your sources stay beside the ideas they inspire.不必把思路放在这里，把资料放在那里。
文字、文稿、媒体与完整导图，在同一张画布上建立连接。

can be a node.思维导图，
也能成为节点。

02 / THINK IN EQUATIONS

Beautiful math.
Clearer thinking.让复杂，
清晰而优雅。

Equations belong in the flow of your thinking, not in a separate file. Write inline or multiline LaTeX alongside your words. Fractions, matrices, aligned equations, and cases—with editable TeX underneath.公式不是思路之外的附件。
分式、矩阵、对齐推导、分段表达式，
和文字一起，进入主题。
行内或多行 LaTeX，始终保留可编辑源码。
