Internal test records: October 9–12, 2026内部测试记录：2026 年 10 月 9–12 日

These results describe specific development checks, not an independent benchmark or a promise for every Mac and document. Resources, text complexity, zoom level, and visible node count affect the experience.这些结果来自特定开发检查，并非独立评测，也不是对每台 Mac、每份文稿的承诺。资源、文字复杂度、缩放和可见节点数都会影响体验。

20,000-level structural checks两万层结构检查

A chain with 20,000 levels was checked for finite coordinates, complete node counts, branch moves, structure changes, and undo/redo equivalence. The October 12 record contains 20,011 structural assertions. A separate native-window check verified that focusing far down the chain and reversing the layout retained visible nodes.两万层深链检查覆盖坐标有效性、节点完整性、分支移动、结构改变及撤销/重做一致性。10 月 12 日记录包含 20,011 项结构断言；另有原生窗口检查，验证远处聚焦和反向布局后仍有节点可见。

This does not mean 20,000 nodes render smoothly at once. A dense full-map view still has noticeable delays.这不意味着两万节点同时显示仍然流畅。密集全景视野仍有明显延迟。

Long-topic loading: about 52% less time长主题加载耗时降低约 52%

On an Apple M5 Pro with 24 GiB memory and macOS 27.0.1, optimized test binaries used identical test scheduling and three independent before/after runs, taking medians. A long-topic loading scenario in a 1,000-node document with 5,000 source paragraphs changed from 1,344.653 ms to 642.035 ms, a reduction of approximately 52.3% versus the earlier implementation. It is a local loading-call measurement, not key-to-screen latency.使用 Apple M5 Pro、24 GiB 内存、macOS 27.0.1；优化测试程序采用相同调度，运行三组独立前后对照并取中位数。1,000 节点文稿中的 5,000 源段落长主题加载，由 1,344.653 ms 降至 642.035 ms，较此前实现降低约 52.3%。这是本地加载调用耗时，不是按键到屏幕的延迟。

In a separate 10,000-node / 5,000-paragraph scenario, combined store initialization and native editor creation changed from 1,924.269 ms to 1,105.765 ms, approximately 42.5% less time. First editing and paragraph splitting at this text size still exceed a 10 ms target.另一组 10,000 节点 / 5,000 段落场景中，文稿初始化与原生编辑器创建合计由 1,924.269 ms 降至 1,105.765 ms，降低约 42.5%。此文字规模的首次编辑和段落拆分仍未达到 10 ms 目标。

Dense drawing remains a work in progress密集绘制仍在优化

The October 12 CPU raster-output test used a 1,200 × 800 non-Retina canvas, 20 frames, and four warm-up frames. It includes background, grid, drawing, and ImageRenderer output; it is not physical-screen FPS or touch-event latency.10 月 12 日 CPU 栅格输出测试采用 1,200 × 800 非 Retina 画布、20 帧、前 4 帧预热，包含背景、网格、绘制和 ImageRenderer 输出；不等于物理屏幕 FPS 或触控事件延迟。
